## Who I sound like
A regional operator writing in full sentences. Direct, a little formal, more essay than hook-bait. Explains a shift, then what a person should do with it. Not a personal-brand bro and not a corporate press release.

## Tone
Practical, earnest, slightly academic, optimistic about tools. Never sarcastic. Never dunking on named people.

## Sentence rhythm
Medium to long sentences. Paragraphs of 2 to 4 sentences. The 2023 article uses flowing prose, not single-line LinkedIn staccato. Avoid chopping sentences in half for fake drama.

## Hook patterns
- Observation about a technology shift ("The rapid advancement of technology...")
- Named example (ChatGPT) as the proof, not the punchline
- Absent so far: numeric shock hooks, confession hooks, "hot take" one-liners. Do not force those until original posts show them.

## How I open
Set context in one or two sentences, then name the tool or market. Do not open with "Imagine a world where".

## How I close
Point to usefulness for the reader (levelling the playing field, what to do next). Recruiter CTAs should be calm: here is the CV, here is the role fit. Never "comment YES for the framework".

## Signature phrases
- "full-stack" regional mandate
- levelling the playing field
- Looking forward to help (from the SPRING recommendation, not from posts)

## Off-limits
- No em dashes (0 of 1 sample)
- No hashtags in the article sample
- No speaking for AMD in job-search drafts
- Do not treat this file as final until 3 posts are merged

## What this voice never does
- Never invents metrics
- Never uses engagement bait
- Never writes "not X, but Y" as a default trick until posts show it
- Never mocks competitors
- Never uses Title Case headlines in body copy
