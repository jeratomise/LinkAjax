# Cover Letter Sample

Source: Jerome_Ng_CoverLetter_Cursor_HeadAPJFieldMarketing.docx (ingested 16/08/2026)
Use this as a voice and structure reference for generated cover letters.

## Structure observed

1. Header with contact info
2. Role reference line (Re: [Role Title], [Company])
3. Opening paragraph: current role mandate, why this role maps directly
4. Proof section with 3-4 bullet points, each with a bold label
5. Closing paragraphs: motivation, cultural fit, call to action
6. Sign-off

## Voice markers

- Direct and confident without being boastful
- Uses quantified proof where available
- Acknowledges company culture and mission
- Explains what draws him to the role (not just qualifications)
- British English spelling (specialising, recognising, theatre)
- No em dashes (uses commas and dashes appropriately)
