# Example job description

Use this to test `npm run apply -- --jd data/applications/_example/jd.md`.

## Role
Director of Product Marketing, Cloud and AI Infrastructure, APAC

## Company
A global semiconductor and infrastructure company hiring in Singapore.

## The job
- Own APAC product marketing for cloud, server, and AI infrastructure
- Turn technical roadmaps into regional campaigns, partner plays, and sales enablement
- Work with country teams in Southeast Asia, Korea, India, and Australia
- Use modern AI tooling to speed research, content, and reporting without losing judgement
- Hire and develop a small GTM pod

## Requirements
- 10+ years in B2B or technology marketing, APAC experience required
- Cloud, data centre, or semiconductor GTM preferred
- Evidence of shipped campaigns and internal or industry recognition
- Comfortable with executives, partners, and technical sellers
- English required, Mandarin a plus
- Degree in economics, business, or related field

## Nice to have
- Experience with AMD, Intel, NVIDIA, hyperscalers, or OEM/ODM channels
- Awards in regional marketing
- AI coursework or applied AI in a marketing org
