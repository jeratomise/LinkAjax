Speed without an editor is just a faster way to ship a wrong claim.

B2B marketing is a claims business. Product names. Proof points. Who can say what, in which market, with which partner. AI will happily remix last quarter's sentence into this quarter's banner. If nobody checks it against a source of truth, the team has not become more productive. It has become more exposed.

The useful pattern is an exception router, not a publish button. Extract the claims. Compare them to the approved list. Pass the clean work. Send only the mismatches to a person.

That person is the editor. In a serious team, it is a named role, even if it sits inside the same marketer's week. Editorial control is also how a lot of public text stays inside the human-review carve-out now that transparency rules are in force in the EU. The legal detail is for counsel. The operating lesson is already clear.

If AI drafts, a human still owns the sentence.

Recruiters looking for senior GTM talent are not hiring for prompt volume. They are hiring for people who can keep a regional machine fast without letting the claims drift.
