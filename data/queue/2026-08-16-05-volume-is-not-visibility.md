A content factory is a comforting response to AI. If drafts are cheap, ship more of them. Cover every keyword. Fill every surface.

Buyers are not waiting on your homepage for that volume to arrive.

More of the research now happens as a question to an answer engine. The engine returns a shortlist. The shortlist is built from pages it can trust, structure it can lift, and names it can attribute. Thin posts, multiplied, teach the machine that you have little to say.

The productivity move is the opposite of a factory. Fewer pieces. Sharper frames. A named point of view. Proof a sceptical operator can check. Structure that can be cited without being rewritten into mush.

That is slower at the keyboard and faster in the market. It also happens to be how a marketer should look on LinkedIn if the goal is a senior GTM role. Not more posts. A body of work a hiring manager could quote.

AI can help you reach that standard. It cannot invent the standard for you.
