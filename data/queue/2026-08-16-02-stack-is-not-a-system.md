Buying another AI tool is the easiest way to feel modern. It is a weak way to raise the productivity of a marketing trade.

A stack is a list of logins. A system is a sequence that the team can repeat on a Tuesday, under time pressure, without a hero.

The sequence I trust is ordinary. Plan the motion. Write the brief. Produce the first version. Approve against a source of truth. Publish. Then learn, in writing, what the market did with it.

AI belongs inside those stages. It does not replace them. Research can be compressed. Drafts can be multiplied. Reporting can be summarised. Approval still needs a named person. Learning still needs a decision, not a dashboard screenshot.

Teams that skip the spine collect prompts the way they used to collect agencies. Lots of activity. Little compounding.

If you lead regional GTM, the question to ask this quarter is not which model the team prefers. It is which stage still depends on memory, Slack, and whoever stayed late.

That is the work worth hiring for.
