The least interesting use of AI in B2B marketing is watching it write a paragraph.

The useful use is compressing the groundwork that used to eat the week. Account context. Partner motion. Competitive claims. What a Korea enterprise buyer will ask that a Singapore channel partner will not.

That work was always the job. It was just slow, so teams skipped it and hoped the global deck would travel.

AI is good at gathering a first map. It is poor at deciding which map is true for this market, this quarter, this route to revenue. That call still sits with the person who has to live with the pipeline number.

So the operating rule is simple to say and hard to keep. Use the model to arrive at the meeting prepared. Do not use it to skip having a point of view.

For a regional marketer, that is the productivity story that holds up in an interview. Not "we generated more assets". We spent the hours we recovered on the market, the partner, and the proof.

That is the trade. Faster groundwork. Same standard of judgement.
